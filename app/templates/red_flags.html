Job Scam & Ghost Listing Red Flags Checklist

Understand the critical differences between Fee/Impersonation Scams and Ghost Listings / Resume Harvesting.

Section 1: Fee Scams & Brand Impersonation

Scams designed to extort money directly from job seekers through fake job offers.

1 Upfront Fees & Security Deposits

Demanding "Registration Fees", "Security Deposits", "Processing Fees", or "Training Kit Charges" before or upon joining is 100% fraudulent. Real employers pay you; you never pay them.

2 Instant Unsolicited Selection

Receiving an offer letter over WhatsApp or SMS claiming instant selection for TCS, Infosys, or Wipro without formal multi-round technical interviews is a scam tactic.

3 Recruiters Using @gmail.com or @yahoo.com

Corporate recruiters send official communication strictly from verified company domain emails (e.g. @accenture.com). Anyone emailing from public free email services is impersonating the brand.

4 Telegram "Prepaid Task" Earning Scams

Promising ₹5,000 daily for liking YouTube videos or completing Telegram tasks, followed by requiring ₹3,000 "crypto deposits" to release earned money.

Section 2: Ghost Listings & Data Harvesting

Phantom job postings created to collect applicant data for AI training or data resale without hiring intent.

1 Continuous Reposting Over Months

The exact same job title at a company is refreshed every few weeks for 3–6+ months continuously. Companies keep ghost listings open to create a false impression of corporate growth or build resume pipelines.

2 Disproportionate Initial Application Data

Mandating scans of Aadhaar cards, PAN cards, full home address, bank statements, or salary slip history at the initial resume submission phase before any shortlist or interview occurs.

3 Applying via Google Forms / Typeform

Legitimate companies use established ATS portals (Greenhouse, Lever, Workday, Ashby, SmartRecruiters). Listings routing candidates to unverified Google Forms harvest candidate emails and phone numbers for spam databases.

4 Generic Vague Job Responsibilities

Listings with zero specific project requirements, extremely vague duties ("perform assigned daily tasks"), and wide salary ranges designed to attract maximum resume submissions across all skill levels.
